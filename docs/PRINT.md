# Print kit for the booth (Sunday 15 Nov)

**Who:** whoever arranges printing for Team Hannah Family. **When:** printed and at our table by **Sunday 08:30**
(tonight if possible).

## Why we print
At the booth, people take a **phone photo of a printed drawing** and our system answers in seconds: the area appears,
with the room outlined on the photo. Printed sheets make the demo real: site engineers work from paper.

The six drawing pages are:

Each page carries a one-line credit at the bottom. **Keep it on the print**, because the licences require it.
