# QA "phone" - design-lead review, 390x844 + 430x932, English

Screenshots: /home/claude/critique/qa-phone/ (`390-*`/`430-*` = page scrolls, `f-*` = flows, `robin-*` = Robin persona). 430 looks the same as 390 (no extra issues), so only 390 is cited. Overall: the payday hero, the trend line, the 30-day chart and the mortgage example come close to Monzo/Revolut quality. What still looks developer-made is (a) the same idea shown with different numbers on different pages, (b) every page uses its own card, button and disclosure style, and (c) several raw forms, native selects and jargon strings.

## P1 - broken, confusing or ugly

1. **Coach, every typed question**: an orange "Quick answers mode – the AI coach isn't available…" box with 3 alternative chips shows above the answer, even when the typed text is word-for-word a starter question and the answer below it is correct (`f-cu-where-0`, `f-cu-safe-0`, `f-cu-save-0`). It reads as an error on every turn. *Fix*: when the match is exact or high-confidence, show no notice. Otherwise use one grey line ("Quick answers - showing the closest match") and no chips. `answer-card.js:12,207` (`.quick-notice`).
2. **Home hero headline and its legend don't add up**: the hero says "€38 a day", the legend says "Free to spend €266", and "Why this number" shows that €38 = (basics €311 + free €266)/15 (`f-why-1`). The legend says "Buffer €705", but the breakdown lists "Safety cushion €500" and "Surprise buffer €204.58" as separate rows. *Fix*: use the same labels and amounts in the legend and the breakdown (split Buffer into cushion + surprise, or merge them in both). Change the sub-line to "a day until payday (€577 in total)". `home-v4.js` (`.hv4-legend`, `why.*`).
3. **Three different "buffer" numbers across pages**: Payday plan sets aside €204.58 "this payday" (`f-why-1`). The coach card says "Put €400 in your surprise buffer", and the Surprise buffer card says €1,200 / €400 a month (`390-home-1`). The Goals card says "We keep €500.00 untouched as your safety cushion" (`390-goals-3`). A user can't tell which one is real. *Fix*: use one buffer model and one number. Show the €400 figure on Home only once (drop either the coach card CTA or the buffer card CTA).
4. **Robin (runs short): the hero math breaks**: it shows €0/day on a celebratory green card. The segmented bar is drawn from €853+€541+€139 = €1,533 while the account holds €312, so the proportions mean nothing (`robin-home-0`). The breakdown line reads "€139.35 over 15 days is about €0.00 a day" (`robin-home-1`). The shortfall is €1,220.72 here but −€773 in the 30-day chart (`robin-fc`). *Fix*: switch the card to a negative state (amber, headline "€1,221 short before payday", primary CTA "See what is due"). Hide the bar or scale it to bills vs balance, drop the per-day sentence when the result is ≤0, and state on both pages whether the €500 cushion is included.
5. **Coach "safe to spend" answer donut** has no labels, no legend, no centre value and one muted colour (`f-c-safe-0`, `f-cu-safe-1`). Nobody can read it. *Fix*: use the same coloured segmented bar + legend as the Home hero, or remove it. `answer-card.js` chart kind `ring`.
6. **Spending "Adjust the assumptions" is a raw form** (`f-sp-assume-0/1`): two fieldsets are both titled "Must-pays", with no merchant names. Amounts are negative ("-528.38") and every block has an "Include in this estimate" checkbox. The button "Recalculate preview ›" is followed by the jargon "ledger and saved rules", plus a second "How we calculated this" disclosure. *Fix*: list rows as "Rent · €528 · 5th [toggle]" with positive amounts and an edit pencil, have one "Update chart" button, and use plain copy. `forecast-v5.js` / `#forecast-assumptions`.
7. **Goals page order is upside down** (`390-goals-0..3`): the H1 is "Start with a wish." but the first content is two optional Plans. The wish form comes next, and the user's existing goal (Italy) is at the very bottom, below an accordion. *Fix*: order it as existing goals first (or the wish input when there are none), then "Plans" and the detailed planner. Rename the H1 to "Goals" with the wish line as a subtitle. `index.html #goals-view`, `plans.js` (`#plans-root`).
8. **Goal card contradicts itself**: "€500.00 a month" next to "you have about €195.51 left each month" (`390-goals-3`), with no warning that the plan doesn't fit. *Fix*: add a status line ("€305 more than you usually have left - extend to Jul 2027?") with an action.

## P2 - polish and consistency

9. **Buttons**: there are 4 filled-green primary buttons on Home alone. Primary labels are centred on Home ("Set aside €400 monthly") but left-aligned on Me/Goals ("Start the intro", "Add something", "Keep this wish"). The "Turn on" plan buttons are full-width outlined with left-aligned text, so they look like input fields (`390-goals-0`). *Fix*: use one primary per screen, centre labels everywhere, and make "Turn on" a switch or a compact secondary button. `styles.css .primary-button`, `plans-v5.css`.
10. **The two plan examples behave differently** (`f-g-mex-0..3` vs `f-g-tex-0..2`). The mortgage example replaces the card and closes with "Close the example" at the bottom, with "Use my own numbers ›" plus a chevron. The tax example appends a dashed box, has both an "×" and a "Close example" link above it, and its CTA has no chevron. Tax shows an "Off" pill, mortgage doesn't. Tax uses €3,100.00-style decimals, mortgage uses €63,694. *Fix*: use one example pattern for both. `plans-tax.js:58`, `plans-mortgage.js:379`.
11. **Disclosure patterns**: native `▶ How we calculated this` (trend), a link-styled "How is this worked out?" (forecast), "How we worked this out ›" (goal) and "Why this number? ⌄" (hero). *Fix*: one style with a chevron row, and one wording.
12. **Number formatting**: decimals vary ("€500.00", "€3,000.00 target" on Goals vs "€3,000" on Home vs "€1,878.66" on Spending). Axis labels use "€3K" on trend but "€6k" on forecast. "Sept" vs "Sep". "20 %" in English (`f-cu-save-0`). "1 y 6 mo sooner" (`f-g-mex-2`). *Fix*: drop cents everywhere except transaction rows, use lowercase "k", and write "1 year 6 months".
13. **Spending filters**: the basket chips sit *below* the list they filter, and the trend chart ignores the chosen basket (`f-sp-leaks-0/1`). "3/6/12 months" sits above the donut but only changes the trend chart, while the donut stays "this month" (`f-sp-3m-0`). *Fix*: move the range control into the trend card and the basket chips above the ring, and have the trend follow the basket.
14. **Transactions sheet** (`f-sp-cat-0`): the meta line "· 5 rows · Observed", "Groceries" repeated as every row title, an "Open transaction detail ›" link on every row, slight per-row x-misalignment, and the footer "Connected transaction details belong to S-mobiili." *Fix*: show merchant + date with the amount right-aligned and the whole row tappable, and a header "5 purchases · €361". `i18n.js:564`.
15. **Context chip and sheet**: the chip is missing on Home but present on Spending/Goals/Me, and Coach uses a third style (`390-coach-0`). The sheet is titled "Customer and month" but has no month field when opened from the chip (`f-sheet` vs `f-avatar`). It also shows "CUST00001", a bare "Edit" link and native selects. Topic sheet close (big X in a filled circle, `f-c-topics`) ≠ other sheets' close.
16. **Header**: a native "English ▾" select takes the most prominent header spot on every page. *Fix*: move it into Me/settings or make it a compact globe icon.
17. **Card heading sizes vary**: "Your balance over the next 30 days" (~22px heavy) vs "Where it went"/"How spending changes over time" (~18px). The goal card uses an olive background and the "Make the plan yours" card a dashed border (`390-home-2`, `390-goals-3`). *Fix*: one card token set (white, 20px radius) and one H2 size.
18. **Coach layout**: the floating "Browse topics" pill overlaps the bottom of the conversation (`f-cu-safe-1`). Header + composer + nav leave ~520px for chat. "What are my Little leaks?" has a stray capital.

## P3 - nice to have

19. Me opens with a big "Your coach doesn't know you yet" card, then "Taxes" (a duplicate of Goals), then 4 Keep/Not this cards. Salary shows €2,365 here, €2,410 on Spending and €2,448 on Home. Add a profile header (name, account) and group the questions as one "4 things to confirm" stepper.
20. On the 30-day chart, label the dotted "today" line and put the date ticks on the axis. The tap tooltip is good.
21. On Home, the "Your 2025 in money is ready" banner and the dashed personalise card at the bottom feel like leftovers. Merge them into one "For you" row.
22. On the mortgage chart, mark "today" and add a year-by-year table (the "Year by year" heading has nothing under it, `f-g-mex-2`).

## Top 8
1. Coach shows the "AI coach isn't available" warning box on every typed question, even exact matches (answer-card.js quick-notice).
2. Home hero: "€38/day" doesn't match the "Free to spend €266" legend, and the legend's Buffer €705 doesn't match the breakdown rows.
3. Buffer shows as €204.58 / €400 / €500 / €1,200 across Home, the coach card and Goals: pick one model and number.
4. Robin's shortfall state is still a green hero at €0/day; the bar is drawn past the balance, "€0.00 a day" reads as nonsense, and the shortfall is €1,221 here vs −€773 on Spending.
5. The Coach safe-to-spend donut is unlabeled and single-colour, so it can't be read.
6. "Adjust the assumptions" is a raw developer form: duplicate "Must-pays", negative amounts, jargon.
7. Goals order is upside down (optional plans first, the user's own goal last), and the goal card says €500/mo while only €195 is left.
8. Buttons, disclosures and number formats are inconsistent (4 primaries on Home, left- vs centre-aligned labels, input-like "Turn on", mixed decimals and €3K/€6k, two different plan-example patterns).
