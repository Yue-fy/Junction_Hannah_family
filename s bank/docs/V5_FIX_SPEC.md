Owner decisions that override reviewer suggestions:
- The tab bar stays at the BOTTOM on every screen size (do not move it into the header on desktop).
- The app positioning is the payday plan; categories explain it.

Product decisions for consistency (all lanes follow these):
- **Buffer vocabulary (one name each, EN / FI / SV):** "Safety cushion" / "Turvatyyny" / "Säkerhetsmarginal" = the fixed small reserve never spent (reserve floor). "Surprise buffer" / "Yllätyspuskuri" / "Buffert för oväntat" = money set aside for Surprise must-pays (irregular costs). Nothing else may be called buffer/cushion. "Steady mode" / "Rauhallinen tahti" / "Lugnt tempo" everywhere (never "Careful mode").
- **The daily number** = money for daily life (everyday basics + free to spend) ÷ days to payday. Anything that shows the parts must add up to it visibly (e.g. "Everyday basics €311 + Free to spend €266 = €577 → €38 a day").
- **Money format:** exact cents only where precision matters (balance, transactions, held-back bills, the card headline); whole euros after "about/noin/ungefär", on goal targets, chart labels and summaries. Axis labels: "€1k, €2k" style in EN; "1 000 €" style in FI/SV (no "t." / "tn").
